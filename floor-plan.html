```svg ``` A few things worth being upfront about in this plan. The network zone shown here is generously sized — 11 leaf switches at roughly 1U each and a handful of spine switches would physically fit in far less rack space than the compute racks take, so that zone's footprint is more about giving cabling and airflow room than reflecting actual switch bulk. The CDU sizing is a placeholder too — actual coolant distribution unit count and size for 9 racks at ~120 kW each would come from a real thermal engineering pass, not this sketch. And as flagged earlier, the radiator and solar array systems dwarf the enclosure itself once deployed, so this floor plan only captures the pressurized/structural core, not the true "size" of the operating system in orbit.
